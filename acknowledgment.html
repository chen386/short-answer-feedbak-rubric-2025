Acknowledgment of Understanding

Good feedback acknowledges what the student has done correctly, when applicable, to reinforce their understanding.

  • 3 points: The feedback acknowledges all key correct elements of the response in a way that is meaningful and appropriate to the student’s current understanding.
  • 2 points: The feedback acknowledges some correct elements but misses others, overemphasizes less important aspects, or places the acknowledgment toward the end of the feedback rather than at the beginning.
  • 1 point: The feedback uses vague, general phrases (e.g., “Good job”) that fail to specify what the student did well.
  • 0 points: The feedback incorrectly praises incorrect elements, fails to acknowledge any correct components when present, or offers unwarranted praise relative to the student’s understanding.
